## About me

I'm pursuing an MSc in Digital Financial Technology at the National University of Singapore, with an undergraduate background in Supply Chain Management at Macau University of Science and Technology.

My projects connect business problems with data and software: organizing research with local language models, testing systematic trading ideas, and designing blockchain-based workflows. I'm interested in how AI and financial technology can make everyday analytical work more useful and easier to navigate.

## Selected projects

### [Research Reading Agent](https://github.com/lyd152289317402021-star/reading-agent)
**Python · Ollama · OCR · Obsidian**

A local research assistant that brings company announcements, PDFs, images, and notes into a structured reading workflow.

- Collects announcements and organizes material by research topic.
- Produces source-linked summaries and daily updates.
- Keeps review and interpretation in the user's hands.

### [FX Trend Strategy](https://github.com/lyd152289317402021-star/FT5010)
**Python · pandas · OANDA API · Plotly Dash**

A course project combining trend, reversal, and volatility signals across seven major USD currency pairs.

- Connects strategy research and backtesting with a practice-account execution workflow.
- Includes position sizing, risk controls, and live monitoring.
- Provides a dashboard for reviewing strategy behavior and execution state.

[View the dashboard repository →](https://github.com/lyd152289317402021-star/FT5010-Dashboard)

### [InnovateDAO](https://github.com/ShengShuYan/InnovateDAO)
**Solidity · Hardhat · OpenZeppelin · MetaMask**

A team blockchain project demonstrating NFT-based membership and on-chain governance, from wallet connection and vote delegation to proposal execution and refunds.

- Supports a local end-to-end flow: mint membership NFT, delegate votes, submit a proposal, vote, and execute or claim a refund.
- My focus: testing, validation, and documentation of the governance workflow.
- The linked repository is the original team repository maintained by ShengShuYan.
